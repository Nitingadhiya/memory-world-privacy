Privacy Policy

Memory World is a memory game for children, built to collect nothing.

1.What we collect

Nothing. Memory World does not collect, send or share any personal information.

There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

The app does not ask for the internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

2.What stays on the device

To remember a child’s game, the app saves the following on the device only:

  • Levels finished, stars and coins
  • Daily Challenge and Daily Reward progress
  • Awards and simple play statistics (games played, play time today)
  • Settings such as sound and the daily play limit

This information never leaves the device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

3.Grown-up areas

Settings and the parent area sit behind a question for grown-ups.

The app has no purchases today. If purchases are added later, they will also sit behind this question and this policy will be updated first.

4.Third-party services

None. The app uses open-source code libraries that run on the device only and do not collect data.

5.Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the app update and on the store listing, with a new effective date.

6.Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or your child’s privacy, please get in touch.
